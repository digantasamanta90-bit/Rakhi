No generic AI gradients everywhere.Use a controlled palette with one dominant background and carefully chosen accents.
No random floating objects.Every flower, particle, thread, portrait and card must have a reason to exist.
No excessive glassmorphism.One or two translucent surfaces maximum where needed.
No generic motivational quote typography.
No giant glowing headings with five different fonts. 💀
No symmetrical "everything perfectly centred" layouts in every scene.
We'll deliberately use asymmetry. Portrait on one side, typography on another. A thread travelling across unexpected paths. Some scenes almost empty.
Palette direction
I wouldn't lock exact colours yet, but broadly:
Deep plum / wine for cinematic depth
Warm ivory / cream for softness
Muted rose or dusty pink for warmth
Antique gold for festive highlights
Deep chocolate brown specifically around Scene 4
Muted lavender/violet for some dreamy digital moments
Importantly: muted and sophisticated, not maximum saturation.
Her animated portraits
This is probably the biggest thing preventing the site from feeling generic.
We should convert her pictures into a consistent editorial illustration style, not a generic Pixar/cartoon style.
Think:
expressive but elegant
slightly painterly or illustrated
clean silhouette
controlled details
same lighting language across every image
subtle imperfections and texture
The portraits should look like they belong to an art-directed visual story.
Animation style
Smooth, restrained, and deliberate.
Avoid:
everything bouncing
endless floating cards
exaggerated zoom-ins
particles exploding every five seconds
constant motion
Prefer:
slow reveals
masked text reveals
objects entering along curved paths
thread drawing animations
gentle camera movement
layered parallax
occasional dramatic transitions
Basically, the page should often know when to shut up and be still.
Typography
Maximum two font families.
One expressive display font for:
Anwesha's name
major Rakhi greetings
final emotional moments
One clean modern font for:
jokes
system messages
normal text
buttons
cards
Typography itself should carry a lot of the aesthetic.
